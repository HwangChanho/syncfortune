# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Last updated: 2026-08-31**

This policy follows the Korean *Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce*.
Where this policy conflicts with the law, the law prevails.

## 1. Paid currency "woon"

Paid content is unlocked with **woon**, spent in units. Because woon is consumed piece by piece,
it is **divisible digital content**, and **unspent woon counts as content not yet delivered**.

## 2. Refunds

### 2.1 Purchased on the web

| | |
|---|---|
| **Window** | **Within 7 days** of payment |
| **Scope** | **Unspent woon only** |
| **Amount** | Pro rata (e.g. 600 woon for ₩36,000 with 200 left → ₩12,000) |
| **Method** | Reversed to the original payment method |

> ⚠️ **Woon already spent is not refundable** — that content has been delivered.

### 2.2 Purchased in the app (App Store · Google Play)

Billing, cancellation and refunds follow **each store's policy**. Please contact the store directly.
We do not hold your store payment details and cannot reverse those charges ourselves.

### 2.3 Woon received for free

Woon given through events, bonuses, gifts or coupons is **not refundable**.

## 3. Minors

A purchase made by someone **under 19** without their legal guardian's consent **may be cancelled**
(Korean Civil Act art. 5), regardless of the 7-day window above.

## 4. How to request

Use **Settings → Contact** in the app or web with your **payment date and order number**.
We respond within **3 business days**.

## 5. If the service closes

Remaining **paid** woon will be refunded as announced in the closure notice.
